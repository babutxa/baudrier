Baudrier Remote Setup

How you would chat with Baudrier from your phone, and on which device each part runs. The model, the agent and your notes all stay on your Mac.

Where each part runs

DeviceWhat runs there
Your phone A browser with the chat page new, the Tailscale app new, later the Dropbox app later. No app to build.
Your Mac Tailscale new, server.py new, agent.py, tools.py, the Apertus model and workspace/. Later the watcher for _inbox later.
Internet Tailscale servers (login and keys only), Open-Meteo (weather), later Dropbox (your documents).

One message, step by step

  1. You type a question in the chat page on your phone.
  2. The browser sends POST /chat through the Tailscale network to your Mac.
  3. server.py calls agent.ask() with your text.
  4. The agent runs Apertus and the tools in a loop until it has an answer.
  5. The answer goes back to server.py, then through Tailscale to your phone.
  6. The conversation stays in the agent, so your next question can refer to it.

What stays private

  • The model, the agent and your notes never leave your Mac.
  • Nothing is open to the internet: only your own devices can reach port 8000.
  • Tailscale's servers help your devices find each other; your chat travels encrypted between them.
  • Your MacBook must be awake. For the family manager, an always-on Mac is better.
